# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

Let It Go doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The app and its Safari extension have no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no accounts.
- **Searches stay in Safari.** The extension only looks at searches on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo and Ecosia, and only to tell whether a search is a `go/` link. Searches that aren't go/ links are left alone. Nothing about your searches is recorded or sent anywhere.
- **Your settings stay on your device.** The redirect address you choose, and the list of search engines you've allowed the extension on, are saved on your Mac, iPhone or iPad and never leave it.
- **Where go/ links go.** When you open a `go/` link, Safari loads it from the redirect address you set (`http://go` by default). That server is yours or your organization's, not ours, and its own privacy policy applies.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions are welcome as [issues on GitHub](https://github.com/Nicolapps/let-it-go/issues).
