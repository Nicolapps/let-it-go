# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 23, 2026_

Let It Go doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The app, its Safari extension and the Chrome extension have no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no accounts.
- **Searches stay in your browser.** The extension only looks at searches on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo and Ecosia, and only to tell whether a search is a `go/` link. Searches that aren't go/ links are left alone. Nothing about your searches is recorded or sent anywhere.
- **Your settings stay on your device.** The redirect address you choose, and the list of search engines you've allowed the extension on, are saved on your Mac, iPhone or iPad and never leave it. In Chrome, the redirect address is saved in your browser profile on this computer, and isn't synced.
- **Where go/ links go.** When you open a `go/` link, your browser loads it from the redirect address you set (`http://go` by default). That server is yours or your organization's, not ours, and its own privacy policy applies.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions are welcome as [issues on GitHub](https://github.com/Nicolapps/let-it-go/issues).
